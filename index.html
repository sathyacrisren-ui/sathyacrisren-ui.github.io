The app, frame by frame

What nothing else can say

Your week, planned together.

Lifts placed clear of the long run, rehab on the quiet days — and it tells you why. One model spans all three — something a single-sport app cannot compute.

Fitness, fatigue and form — on everything.

The model endurance athletes swear by, computed on your lifting, your runs, your rides and your rehab. The apps runners love can’t feel a squat.

Findings, not dashboards.

A statistics engine on your phone lines your own days up — sleep against top sets, carbs against pace — and speaks only when a link clears every gate. Every finding shows its evidence. The AI phrases; it never invents.

Rehab that knows what you lifted.

Criteria-gated phases, your physio’s plan as the prescription of record, and the camera as a knee protractor. Progress never stops — it redirects.

On your wrist.

Start, finish and log sets from your Apple Watch — reps, weight and effort prefilled, the rest timer already counting from the moment you rack the bar. The countdown follows you to the lock screen.

A race money can’t win.

The weekly duel scores witnessed showing-up only — a friend in knee rehab can beat a bodybuilder by being consistent. One link races a whole group.

What it refuses to do

  • No guilt streaks. A rest day is part of the programme, not a failure.
  • No fake urgency. Countdowns only for real dates. Nothing expires.
  • No buyable progress. Points pay for information only, and can never be bought.
  • No AI theatre. If the engine didn’t prove it, the app doesn’t say it.
  • No “we miss you.” Notifications are budgeted — you choose how often.
  • Your data stays on your phone. The analysis runs there too.

Free at launch

The core loop is free, permanently: sessions, findings, the race, rehab scorekeeping. Premium comes later and gates depth — never the loop.
